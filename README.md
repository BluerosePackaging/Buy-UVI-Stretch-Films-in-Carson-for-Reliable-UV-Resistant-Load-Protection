## What Makes UVI Stretch Film Different?

UVI stands for UV-inhibited stretch film. The film incorporates UV-resistant properties intended to help it maintain its performance when exposed to sunlight for longer periods than standard stretch film.

This makes it useful for palletized products that may be stored outside, transported through sunny environments, or temporarily staged in areas where direct UV exposure is unavoidable.
